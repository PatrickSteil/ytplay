# Autodj Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** With autodj on, the next song starts under the current one: beat-matched over 4 bars when ytplay is very sure, otherwise a plain crossfade.

**Architecture:** `analyze` finds, per song, the audible start/end and a beat grid for its first and last 40 s with a sureness verdict. `autodj` turns two songs' analyses into a `Plan` (pure) and can render it offline. `player` carries the plan out on its two decks. Tasks 1 to 5 can be heard and tested without the live engine; the live engine (6 to 8) starts after the user has listened to the rendered previews.

**Tech Stack:** Go 1.26, plain-Go DSP (no new dependencies); ffmpeg only in the gated preview test.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-autodj-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Sure means all of: sharpness at least 5, beat offset in each quarter of the 40 s stretch within 20 ms of each other, peak at least 1.5 times the opposite phase, local tempo within 0.5% of the song's BPM.
- Beat-match only if both ends are sure and the tempi are within 6%; overlap 16 beats; ease the speed back over 8 s; crossfade 6 s (or the song's own length if under 15 s); equal-power volume curves.
- Envelope timing bias is calibrated on synthetic clicks, not hard-coded without a test that checks it.
- Songs with no BPM have no grids. Analysis of the ends must add under about 1.5 s to a four-minute song.
- No new dependencies. Tests must not need mpv, ffmpeg or the network, except the gated corpus and preview tests (`YTPLAY_CORPUS`).
- `make check` passes at the end of every task. Do not commit unless the user asks.

## Review Focus

- A song with silence before or after the music: anchors and the plan use the audible part, not the file's length (Tasks 1, 4).
- A song whose beat only starts after a beatless intro, or whose tempo wanders: never a confident wrong grid; it refuses (Tasks 2, 3).
- Off-beat hats or guitars as loud as the kick: the grid sits on the kick (Task 2).
- Two songs with very different tempi, half/double-time pairs, or a song too short for 16 beats: a crossfade (Task 4).
- Missing analysis for either song: a crossfade by duration, never a crash or a wrong beat-match (Tasks 4, 6).

### Task 2: a beat grid for one stretch

**Files:**
- Modify: `internal/analyze/edges.go`; Test: `internal/analyze/edges_test.go`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `eachFrame` (analyze.go).
- Produces: `func gridOf(x []float32, bpm float64) gridFit` where `x` is the stretch and `type gridFit struct { period, phase, sharp, wobble, margin float64 }` (`phase`: seconds from the start of `x` to a beat, bias-corrected, in `[0, period)`); a test helper `drumTrack(bpm, firstBeat, seconds float64, opt drumOpts) []float32` (kick on every beat; optional off-beat hats louder than the kick, beatless pad intro, per-beat jitter, silence at both ends) with the true beat times returned by `drumBeats(...)`.
- Rules: fine onset envelope per band (30-200, 200-2000, 2000-8000 Hz; window 1024, hop 128), low band weighted 2, others 1; period searched within +-3% of `60/bpm` coarse to fine (about 100 folds, not 1200); beat phase from the folded profile with parabolic interpolation; `wobble` from the beat offset in each quarter of `x` looking only within a quarter beat of the global phase; `margin` is peak over the opposite phase; the timing bias is measured on a synthetic click track in a test and applied as a named constant.

- [ ] **Step 1: Failing tests**: `TestGridOfADrumTrack` (124 BPM, first kick 0.337 s, 40 s: period within 0.1% of 60/124, `phase` within 8 ms of the true beat mod period); `TestGridPhaseBiasIsCalibrated` (clicks at exact multiples of the period: phase within 5 ms); `TestGridIgnoresOffBeatHats` (hats on the off-beat 1.5 times as loud as the kick: phase within 8 ms of the kick, `margin` at least 1.5); `TestGridOfNoiseIsNotSharp` (`sharp` under 3); `TestGridWobbleGrowsWithJitter` (jitter of 40 ms per beat: `wobble` over 20 ms; steady track under 10 ms); `TestGridWithTempoOutsideTheSearchRange` (track at 124, bpm hint 135: `sharp` under 5).
- [ ] **Step 2: Run** → FAIL. **Step 3: Implement** `gridOf` and the helpers above. **Step 4: Run** `go test ./internal/analyze -run Grid` → PASS.
